About

Canadian-born designer, based in Katowice, Poland for over a decade. I started in 2004 in desktop publishing, advertising and graphic design, working freelance with clients across North America, Europe and Asia, before moving in-house into design, marketing and, eventually, product UI/UX.

Today I’m a UI/UX designer at KLDiscovery, working on Nebula, an AI-powered enterprise eDiscovery platform, where I’ve helped shape the design system and visual direction from early product and engineering discussions through to launch.

I work best as a senior design voice, building future-facing solutions together, whether collaborating with product and engineering teams or working directly with clients. I’m hands-on, detail-oriented, and happiest when we’re sketching, talking and building side by side.

Process

Systems thinking, human feeling, thoughtful craft.

Good design doesn't need to shout. It brings people and ideas together and makes interactions feel natural. Thoughtful in structure, intentional in every detail. Design communicates ideas with clarity and purpose.

01

Understand the problem

Start with context before proposing solutions.

02

Structure before detail

Build a clear foundation before refining.

03

Collaborate from the start

Work closely with clients and teams, share ideas early and keep things moving.

04

Communicate with clarity

Make complexity easier to grasp, keep what matters, and leave out the rest.

05

Bring experience

Draw on 20+ years across disciplines to find strong solutions and deliver high quality.

06

Hold the standard

Care about the long-term impact and push back on shortcuts that could hurt the work.
